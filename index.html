08 — Questions founders ask

Before you roll.

What does a storytelling agency do?

We find the one thing only your brand can say, then build your films, ads and content around it, so every piece adds to the same memory in your customer's head.

Do you work with creators and influencers?

Yes. We help creators find the story their page should be built around, then bring them paid deals from D2C brands that fit it. Apply through The Cast section.

Do you only make brand films?

No. We also run Meta performance campaigns, make static and motion ad creatives, and produce UGC. The story decides what we make; the format follows.

What is the free 60-second teardown?

Send us your brand and Instagram or website. We send back a short video showing the story we think you're sitting on. No deck, no sales call unless you ask for one.

Can I see work from my category?

Yes. The work section has 59 pieces across films, UGC, AI campaigns, jewellery and stills. Tell us your category on WhatsApp and we'll send the closest matches from our full archive of 178.

Who do you work with?

D2C brands in India, and the creators they partner with.

Where are you based?

Noida, Uttar Pradesh, working with brands across Delhi NCR and remotely.
